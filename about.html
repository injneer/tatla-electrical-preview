Who we are

Electrical work you can count on

Tatla Electrical Ltd. provides residential, commercial, tenant improvement and low voltage electrical services from our base in Delta, BC.

From a single circuit to a full commercial fit-out, we plan the job with you, give you a clear quote, and handle the work from rough-in to final inspection. We work directly with homeowners, business owners, general contractors and designers.
